# Preparing a PSD for rigging

What a layered PSD needs so it can be rigged well. This is the same preparation human riggers ask for.

## Canvas and size

- Face at least 300 px wide. 500 px or more is better.
- Keep each part as a normal pixel layer. Do not merge parts, and flatten layer effects and
  clipping groups into the layer they belong to.
- One character per file, facing the front, head upright.

## Layers needed

| Part | Layers |
|---|---|
| Eyes, each side | eye white, iris (whole circle, not cut by the lids), highlights, upper lash line, lower lash line, eyelid or crease if drawn |
| Brows | one layer per side |
| Mouth | upper lip line, lower lip line, mouth interior (drawn open), teeth or tongue if wanted |
| Face | face skin, nose, cheeks or blush on their own layers, ears per side |
| Hair | front hair, side locks per side, back hair; separate strands give more depth |
| Body | neck, body, each arm, accessories (hat, ribbons, earrings) on their own layers |

## Paint what is hidden

Parts move, so whatever might come into view must be drawn:

- The face skin continues under the hair and under the brows.
- The neck continues up under the chin.
- The back hair continues behind the head and the body.
- The eye white and the iris are complete under the lids.
- The mouth interior is drawn complete.

## Names

Any clear names work; the rig script maps them. Say which side is which: the character's left
or the screen's left.
